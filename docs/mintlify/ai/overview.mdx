## What your AI can do

## Get started in a minute

No install, no code. Three steps in Claude (web, desktop or phone):

1. Open Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector.
2. Paste the trading address: `https://mcp.veranta.xyz/mcp/trade` (want play money first? `https://testnet-mcp.veranta.xyz/mcp/trade`).
3. Click connect and sign once with your wallet. The key lasts 30 days, can never withdraw, and you can revoke it any time.

Then just talk: "What can I trade?" or "Long 100 USDC of ETH at 3x with a 10 percent stop." The trading address includes every read tool, so one connector is all you need. ChatGPT: the same steps under Settings, Connectors, Developer mode. Other apps and local installs: [Install](/ai/install).

## Two ways to connect

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Hosted: paste one URL">
    Add `https://mcp.veranta.xyz/mcp/trade` as a custom connector in claude.ai, the Claude mobile app or ChatGPT. Your client opens delegate.veranta.xyz once, a session key is created in your browser and your wallet signs one request. OAuth carries the connection to every device that client runs on. Nothing to install, nothing to keep running.

    Want to look before you trade? `https://mcp.veranta.xyz/mcp` answers every market question and shows any wallet's positions with no login at all.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Local: run one command">
    For Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf and VS Code, create a session key on your machine and register it with one wallet signature:

    ```bash
    npx -y veranta-mcp init --network=mainnet --trader=0xYourWallet --expiry=7d
    ```

    Then add the server to your client:

    ```json
    { "mcpServers": { "veranta": { "command": "npx", "args": ["-y", "veranta-mcp"] } } }
    ```

    Your key and your policy file stay with you. Use `--network=testnet` for paper trading with test money.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Then say the trade: "Check my Veranta setup, then long 500 dollars of ETH at 5x with a 10 percent stop." You get the quote. It fills when you say yes. [Install](/ai/install) has the exact steps per client.

## What keeps you safe

- **The key can trade, not withdraw.** Your AI signs with a session key your wallet registers as a delegate. The contract lets it open, close and manage positions and margin (margin withdrawals only ever pay your own wallet). It cannot withdraw USDC from your account, grant allowances or add delegates. You pick the expiry and revoke it any time.
- **Policy limits you choose.** A policy file caps leverage, collateral and notional per trade, notional per day, open positions and daily loss. Presets `conservative`, `standard` and `pro`; hosted runs on `conservative` (3x, 250 dollars per trade, 100 dollars daily loss). The AI cannot edit it.
- **Every trade is quoted, then confirmed.** The quote shows collateral, leverage, notional, fees, liquidation price, warnings and the verdict. It executes once, within 30 seconds, and only after your go-ahead. Trades above your threshold ask for an explicit yes.
- **Pause and daily loss stop.** "Stop trading" blocks new positions while closing keeps working, and only you can resume. The daily loss limit pauses on its own until the next UTC day. Everything the AI does lands in an activity log you can read.
